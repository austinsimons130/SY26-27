#2 Open Up and Say Ahhhh by Poison


This album is listed #2 because it is an album that totally embodies the glam metal energy of the 80s. This is easily Poison's best and most iconic album releasing hits such as Every Rose Has its Thorn and Nothin' But a Good Time. It is an album where I can sing word for word every song.

  • The bands 2nd album released
  • Has the bands top 2 songs
  • Released in 1988
