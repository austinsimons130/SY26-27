#4 Stadium Arcadium by Red Hot Chili Peppers


This album is listed #4 because while it is 28 songs, a really long album, it defines Red Hot Chili Peppers, with many of their hits coming from this album. The Red Hot Chili Peppers experimented with many different sounds as they grew as a band, and this album is one of their more recent albums where they have fully defined their sound. Every song on this album has a good feel and is enjoyable to listen to, and should never be skipped.

  • Released hits like Snow (Hey Oh) and Dani California
  • Bands longest album
  • Released in 2006
