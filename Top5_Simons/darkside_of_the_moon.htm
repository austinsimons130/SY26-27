#1 Darkside of the Moon by Pink Floyd


This album is #1 because it is something I don't listen to often, but when I do listen to it, the music is calming and helps me think. I first listened to it about 2 years ago and have enjoyed it every single time. It is also and album where I never want to skip any songs.

  • The bands most popular album.
  • The bands most popular song is not on this album.
  • Released in 1973
