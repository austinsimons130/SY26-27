#5 When Broken Is Easily Fixed by Silverstein


This album is listed #5 because it's style illustrates the post-hardcore and emo scene. The songs have so much heart put into them, with super catchy and melodic chorus', followed by heavy screamo breakdowns that you can feel in your chest. Every song on the album gets me hyped and is never skipped.

  • The bands first album
  • Features hits like Smashed Into Pieces, Giving Up, and Bleeds No More
  • Released in 2003
