#3 Ocean Avenue by Yellowcard


This album is #3 because it innovates the emo music scene. Although I only recently listened to this album for the first time, it is the only album where every single song was added to my playlist on the first listen. Every single song on the album is super catchy and uses violin even though the songs are heavier, which mixes surprisingly well and replaces lead guitar in many songs. I don't skip songs when listening to the album and love the chatchy chorus' and strong violin solos.

  • Uses violin as a lead instrument
  • Ocean Avenue the song became top song
  • Released in 2003
